Common questions

Does it keep recording with the screen locked?

Yes. Once you tap Start Ride, MotoAtlas records in the background until you tap Stop or Pause, so your phone can stay locked or in its mount.

Where are my rides stored?

On your phone only. They aren't uploaded anywhere.

How do I delete my rider profile?

Rides → Riders → Delete Rider Profile. This deletes your profile, rider list and group rides from our server. Your saved rides stay on your phone.

Why does Max Lean show a dash?

Lean angle is estimated while you're moving through corners above about 18 km/h. Short or straight rides may not record one.
